Pick what you're celebrating

Nine kinds of occasions. Each one sets its own wording, from "Aarav's birthday, turning 5" to "Happy Diwali!" at midnight.

BirthdayCounts to midnight every year and shows the age they turn.
AnniversaryEvery year on the wedding date, with the number of years.
Wedding dayOne countdown to the big day.
New YearAlways set to 1 January. No date to enter.
FestivalDiwali, Eid, Christmas, Pongal or any festival you name.
Baby arrivalCount down to the due date.
GraduationFor the day they walk across the stage.
RetirementMark the last day and the start of the next chapter.
Custom eventA housewarming, a launch or anything with a date.

How it works

  1. Choose the occasion and date

    Pick one of the nine occasions, then type a name and the date. The preview updates as you type.

  2. Share the link

    Send it on WhatsApp or anywhere else. Everyone who opens it sees the same countdown to the same midnight.

  3. Go full screen

    Leave it on a TV, laptop or phone. The last minute shows giant seconds, then the confetti starts.

Made for families in different time zones

Midnight in the right place
Choose whose midnight counts. Cousins in Toronto and grandparents in Chennai reach zero at the same moment, and each person also sees the time on their own clock.
No sign-up
There's no account to create. The name and date live in the link you share and in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.
Works without internet
Add it to your phone's home screen. Once opened, it keeps counting even if the Wi-Fi drops at the party.
Free to use
No ads and no cost. Open it in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, laptop or smart TV.
